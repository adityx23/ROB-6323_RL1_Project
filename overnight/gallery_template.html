Shared training recipe for both runs: 200 demos, two 128 px views (third-person + wrist), action chunks of 10 with 5 executed before replanning, batch 32, 15k steps, AdamW with 5e-5 peak learning rate, 500 warmup steps and cosine decay to 5e-6, gradient clip 1.0, no EMA. π0.5 starts from the pi05_base weights; ACT trains from scratch (ResNet-18 ImageNet backbone).

OCBench: block-double-task2 (stack two cubes), 200 regenerated demos with an image every 5 control steps; 200 seeded eval episodes. Arm actions are deltas from the joint positions at the start of each chunk for both models.
